Your first Sunday at Highfield

Here is what to expect so you can walk in feeling at home.

Where

2407 Egypt, Highfield

Harare. Use the directions link below, or call one of our elders and someone will guide you in.

When

Sunday, 09:00 to 13:00

Bible study runs from 10:00 to 11:00 as part of the Sunday gathering.

What happens

Worship, the Word, prayer

Lively Pentecostal praise and worship, a message from the Bible, and time for prayer. Ushers will help you find a seat.

Questions visitors ask

Good to know

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Many members dress smartly for Sunday, but nobody will turn you away for what you wear.

Is there something for my children?

Yes. Our Children's Ministry teaches kids about Jesus in a way they enjoy. Ask an usher when you arrive.

Where do I park?

Parking details will be added here. For now, ask Deacon Prosper on 0784 584 001.

Will I be asked to give money?

No. Giving is for members who choose to support the church. As a guest, simply enjoy the service.

About us

Pentecostal Assemblies of Zimbabwe, Highfield Assembly

We are one of the PAOZ family of churches, alongside sister assemblies such as Jubilee Christian Center in Milton Park and Hope Community Church.

Our vision

To know Christ, make Him known, and change the world.

Our mission

We are a Bible-based Pentecostal church passionately fulfilling the Great Commission and engaged in social transformation.

Our focus

Igniting the local church for community transformation in Highfield.
